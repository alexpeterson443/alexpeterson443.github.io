Bowling Streak

Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

In short: Bowling Streak does not collect, share or sell any data about you. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone unless you choose to sync it with a server of your own.

What the app stores

The games, scores, frames, paused days and settings you enter are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. A small summary of your streak and recent scores is shared with the app's own widgets on the same device so they can show your streak. This information is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud device backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What the app sends

By default, nothing. Bowling Streak contains no third party code for analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and the developer never receives your data.

Optional server sync

If you turn on Settings › Server sync and enter the address and access key of a Bowling Streak server you run, the app sends your games, frames and paused days to that server and reads them back from it. It talks only to the address you enter. Anything connected to that server, such as a web app or an assistant like Claude that you have given access, can then see and change your log. The access key is stored in your iPhone's keychain. Turning sync off stops all of this; your server keeps the copy it already has, under your control.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone. They are created and delivered on the device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in the Settings app at any time.

Exporting and deleting your data

Settings › Export games creates a CSV file that goes only where you choose to share it. Settings › Erase all data deletes everything from the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
