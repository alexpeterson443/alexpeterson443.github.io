Bowling Streak

Help & Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Send a support request (a free GitHub account is needed). Include your iPhone model and iOS version. Requests are public, so leave out anything private.

How does the streak count?

A day counts once at least one game is logged for it. Your streak is the number of days in a row with a game. Today stays open until midnight, so the streak only breaks if a whole day passes with no game and no pause.

What is a pause?

When you can't bowl (the alley is closed, you're sick or injured, away, or out of time), pause that day from Today or Calendar. A paused day keeps your streak alive but doesn't add to it. You can pause up to 60 days at once, including future days for a trip.

Can I log a game I forgot?

Yes. Open Calendar, tap the day, and choose Add game, or change the date in the Log a game screen. Logging a game before your streak's start date moves the start back.

Frame by frame scoring

Tap the pins knocked down for each ball. Use / for a spare and X for a strike. The app adds up bonus balls and the 10th frame for you, and only lets you enter legal throws. Undo removes the last ball.

Widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and choose Bowling Streak. There are Streak and Scores widgets for the Home Screen and Streak widgets for the Lock Screen.

Syncing with your server and Claude

If you run your own Bowling Streak server, open Settings › Server sync › Connect a server and enter its address and access key. Games and pauses then go to the server and come back from it whenever you open the app or pull down to refresh, and the widgets check it every 30 minutes. Changes made while you're offline wait on the phone and send when you're back online. Anything connected to your server, such as Claude, can then read and update your log.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your log moves with your iPhone or iCloud backup. You can also export a CSV from Settings.
